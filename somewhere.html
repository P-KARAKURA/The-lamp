SOMEWHERE IN AFRICA

Stories beyond the headlines.

Seeing people, places, and everyday life through another lens.

THE IDEA

There is more to a place than what we hear.

Somewhere in Africa is a visual and storytelling space exploring African people, places, cultures, and everyday experiences.

Africa is often introduced to the world through statistics, headlines, conflict, poverty, politics, or extraordinary success stories. But between those extremes are millions of ordinary lives, quiet moments, creative minds, beautiful places, and stories that deserve to be seen.

Through photography, documentaries, and storytelling, Somewhere in Africa looks for those moments.

We want to capture not only what a place looks like, but what it feels like to live there.
