TEA TABLE

Sit. Listen. Share.

Different perspectives. One conversation.

THE IDEA

Some conversations need a table.

Some ideas begin with a lesson. Others begin with a simple conversation.

Tea Table creates room for people to sit together, share experiences, exchange perspectives, ask questions, and discover what can emerge when people are willing to listen.

Across many African communities, questions around gender, family, identity, opportunity, tradition, and social expectations can carry deeply different perspectives.

We do not approach these conversations with the intention of declaring one side right and another wrong.

We listen. We ask. We share.

A woman and a man may experience the same community differently. A young person may see something differently from an elder. Someone raised in one environment may understand a tradition differently from someone raised somewhere else.

Tea Table does not require everyone to think alike. It simply creates a place where different perspectives can meet without judgement, without forcing agreement, and without closing the conversation before it begins.

Sometimes understanding does not begin with changing someone's mind. It begins with listening.

Listen

Make room for experiences and perspectives that may be different from our own.

Share

Speak honestly while respecting the person sitting across the table.

Understand

Look for understanding without requiring everyone to reach the same conclusion.

Pull up a chair.

There is always another perspective worth hearing.
