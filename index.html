ABOUT LAMPSTAND

Lighting Ideas. Connecting Stories. Creating Opportunities.

LAMPSTAND is a space for people, ideas, creativity, and opportunity. We believe that potential can be found everywhere, but sometimes what is missing is a place where potential can meet opportunity.

Through learning, communication, creativity, storytelling, and meaningful conversation, we create spaces where people can discover what they have, express it, connect with others, and turn it into something meaningful.

TEA TABLE

Where Conversations Become Possibilities.

Tea Table is a space created by Lampstand for meaningful conversations, shared experiences, questions, ideas, and human connection.

We believe some of the most important ideas begin when people sit together, listen to one another, and create room for different perspectives.
