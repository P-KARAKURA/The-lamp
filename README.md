# LAMPSTAND

### From Potential to Self-Reliance

> **"We do not only give people opportunities; we help them become capable of creating opportunities."**

Lampstand is a **community-led platform** designed to transform local potential into sustainable self-reliance through practical education, structured pathways, storytelling, dialogue, and creative expression.

---

## 🌍 Vision

A future where communities recognize their potential, build essential capabilities, create opportunities, and achieve sustainable self-reliance.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Lampstand empowers young people and individuals facing limited access to opportunities through:

* Practical education
* Skill development
* Communication and leadership
* Storytelling
* Visual arts and photography
* Community dialogue
* Structured pathways to opportunity
* Partnerships that support sustainable self-reliance

---

## 💡 Our Approach

Lampstand does not seek to define communities only by their challenges.

We believe that communities already possess **knowledge, creativity, experience, determination, and potential**.

Our role is to help create the conditions in which that potential can become capability—and capability can become opportunity.

# 🚀 Program Platforms

## HABLOCLASS

### Speak • Connect • Grow

HABLOCLASS is Lampstand's education, communication, and leadership platform.

Its activities include:

* Public speaking
* Discussion and debate
* Practical learning
* Academic support
* Youth coaching
* Career pathways
* Self-directed learning
* Entrepreneurship and self-reliance

HABLOCLASS seeks to make learning practical, accessible, and connected to real opportunities.

## Somewhere in Africa

### Stories Beyond the Headlines

Somewhere in Africa is a storytelling and visual platform documenting authentic African experiences, people, cultures, places, and everyday life.

The project seeks to present Africa through more than challenges.

It explores:

* People
* Culture
* Creativity
* Resilience
* Everyday life
* Identity
* Beauty
* Contradictions
* Possibilities

Through photography and storytelling, we seek to create a more human and diverse narrative.

---

## Creative Arts & Photography

Creative Arts & Photography provides a space for visual expression, documentation, and skill development.

We believe creativity can help people:

* Tell their stories
* Preserve memories
* Explore identity
* Develop skills
* Build confidence
* Communicate ideas
* Connect with wider audiences

Art is not only decoration.

**It can be a tool for communication, learning, and transformation.**

---

## Tea Table

Tea Table is a platform for dialogue and connection.

It creates spaces where people can:

* Speak openly
* Listen actively
* Exchange ideas
* Ask difficult questions
* Learn from differences
* Build understanding

The objective is not to force agreement.

The objective is to create **respectful conversation without judgment**.

# 🏛️ Institutional Development

Lampstand is being developed as an **institution rather than a personal brand**.

The organization begins with a founding team of three core members. Founding status recognizes the historical contribution of those who establish the initiative while allowing the institution to evolve beyond its founders.

As Lampstand develops, responsibilities may be allocated among:

* Founders
* Organizational leadership
* Program coordinators
* Community contributors
* Volunteers
* Partners
* Advisors
* Future members

The long-term objective is to establish a structure where responsibility is based on **role, competence, accountability, and the needs of the organization**.

---

# ⚖️ Governance & Institutional Protection

Lampstand is intended to operate as an independent institutional entity in accordance with applicable Rwandan laws.

The organization seeks to establish clear procedures for:

* Decision-making
* Financial management
* Authorization of expenditures
* Accountability
* Program management
* Partnerships
* Asset management
* Documentation
* Periodic review and auditing

Organizational assets—including branding, intellectual property, equipment, domains, and organizational capital—are intended to serve the institution and its mission rather than individual founders.

The final legal structure and governance procedures will be developed in accordance with applicable law.

---

# 💰 Financial Accountability

Lampstand aims to maintain transparent and responsible financial practices.
