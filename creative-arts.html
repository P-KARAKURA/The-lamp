THE IDEA

Creativity is another way of speaking.

Creative Arts is a space where imagination becomes expression.

Art can communicate an experience that words cannot easily explain. A photograph can preserve a moment. A painting can carry an emotion. A creative idea can make people see an ordinary subject differently.

We believe creativity is not only something to display. It can help people discover themselves, develop their abilities, communicate ideas, and recognize possibilities they may not have seen before.

LAMPSTAND creates room for different forms of creative expression, while giving talent an opportunity to be seen, developed, and connected.
