HABLOCLASS

Speak. Connect. Grow.

Communication is more than knowing a language.

THE IDEA

The power of expression

HABLOCLASS is a space for communication, expression, and learning.

HABLOCLASS grew from the refugee community, where young people often faced limited access to learning, opportunities, and spaces to express themselves. Through community activities, we supported learners with education, examinations, scholarships, public speaking, dialogue, and personal development. Over time, this experience connected us with educators, community members, organizations, and other partners who shared the same commitment to creating opportunities through learning and communication. These collaborations helped shape HABLOCLASS beyond a local initiative. Today, HABLOCLASS is evolving into a connected learning and opportunity platform—building on what we have learned, continuing existing collaborations, and creating new ones. We often think of language simply as knowing English, French, Kinyarwanda, Kiswahili, or another language. But communication goes beyond vocabulary and grammar.

It is also knowing what to say, how to express it, when to say it, and who needs to hear it.

Our journey began in the community, but our vision reaches beyond it. The limits of your expression can become the limits of your opportunities.

A good idea can remain invisible when it cannot be expressed. A talented person can be overlooked when they cannot communicate their value. An opportunity can pass simply because the right message never reached the right person.

HABLOCLASS seeks to help people develop their voice, communicate with purpose, and expand the connections through which new opportunities can emerge.
