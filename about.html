ABOUT LAMPSTAND

Lighting the way for people, ideas and opportunity.

LAMPSTAND is a refugee-led, community-rooted organization growing from lived experience and a commitment to community-led development. We create spaces where people can turn their knowledge, abilities, ideas and experiences into learning, opportunity, creative expression and meaningful contribution.

OUR MAIN MESSAGE

We believe people should not only wait for opportunities. They should be prepared to create them.

LAMPSTAND brings together learning, creativity, communication, storytelling and practical opportunity in order to help people discover what they already have, develop what they need and use their knowledge in meaningful ways.

Our work grows from community experience, including the realities of displacement, limited opportunities and the determination of people who continue to learn, create, adapt and build despite difficult circumstances.

We are interested not only in helping people receive support, but in helping them develop the confidence, knowledge and skills to become contributors, creators, coaches, entrepreneurs and community leaders.

01 — OUR STORY

Born from experience. Growing through learning, creativity and community.

LAMPSTAND grew from lived community experience and a simple realization: people often have more potential than their circumstances allow them to show.

Its roots can be traced to community learning and efforts to support students as they searched for ways to continue their education and prepare for new opportunities. Over time, those experiences opened a wider question: what happens when learning is connected not only to education, but also to communication, creativity, storytelling, practical skills and self-reliance?

That question continues to shape LAMPSTAND today. We have seen students looking for opportunities, young people searching for direction, entrepreneurs trying to build with limited resources, and artists carrying talent that simply needed a door to open.

One experience that stayed with us was seeing artists from our community given an opportunity through Alight to present and sell their paintings. It showed something important: talent does not always need to be created. Sometimes it needs to be recognized, encouraged and connected to an opportunity.

This is part of the experience from which LAMPSTAND is growing. We are building a platform where learning can lead to confidence, creativity can lead to expression, and opportunity can lead to greater capacity and contribution.

Our Christian foundation also shapes the way we understand this journey. We believe hope is not simply waiting for circumstances to change. Hope can give people the courage to prepare, learn, create, serve and keep building even when the path ahead is uncertain.

02 — OUR MISSION

Turning potential into preparation, opportunity and contribution.

LAMPSTAND exists to help people develop the knowledge, confidence, communication skills and practical abilities they need to participate more fully in their own lives and communities.

We create and connect learning spaces that bring together education, public speaking, coaching, entrepreneurship, creativity, storytelling and access to opportunities. Our aim is not simply to provide information, but to help people turn what they learn into something they can use.

We encourage a culture of self-reliance: learning to think, create, solve problems, communicate ideas and build with the resources available. At the same time, we believe connection matters. The right person, knowledge, opportunity or partnership can open a path that would otherwise remain unseen.

Our Christian foundation gives this mission a deeper sense of purpose. We believe preparation involves the mind, the practical abilities and the spirit — so that people can face difficulty with hope, use opportunities responsibly and become capable of serving others.

03 — OUR VISION

What happens when people lose hope, education, income, belonging and a sense of future?

We envision communities where young people and families can see possibilities beyond the difficulties around them, and where education, skills, opportunity and community support help people build a meaningful direction for their lives.

We want to help create pathways for people who have been pushed away from education, employment or opportunity — including young people, single mothers, entrepreneurs and others who may feel that their future has become too difficult to rebuild.

Our vision is to strengthen self-reliance: people who can learn, speak, create, work, start businesses, support their families, serve their communities and eventually become opportunities for others.

As Christians, we also hold to the hope that circumstances do not have the final word. We believe faith, preparation, community and purposeful action can help people move from despair toward hope, from dependence toward capacity, and from receiving support toward becoming contributors.

04 — OUR OBJECTIVES

Turning hope and potential into practical capacity.

Our objectives are focused on helping people develop the knowledge, confidence, skills and opportunities they need to take greater responsibility for their own future and contribute to the wellbeing of their communities.

Education & Opportunity

Help learners continue their education, prepare for examinations, discover scholarships and navigate opportunities that can open new pathways for their future.

Communication & Public Speaking

Build confidence through dialogue, discussion and debate, helping people develop the ability to think, listen, express ideas and participate meaningfully in society.

Self-Reliance & Practical Skills

Encourage a DIY mindset where people learn to solve problems, create with available resources and develop practical abilities instead of waiting for others to provide every solution.

Entrepreneurship & Livelihoods

Support entrepreneurs with guidance, knowledge and connections as they develop their ideas, strengthen their businesses and work toward greater economic self-reliance.

Community Coaches

Prepare people with useful knowledge and coaching skills so they can serve others, multiply learning and become positive resources within their communities.

Inclusion & New Pathways

Create learning and opportunity pathways for people who may have been left behind, including school dropouts, single mothers, young people and others seeking another chance to rebuild their future.

05 — OUR APPROACH

We prepare people not only to find opportunities, but to be ready for them.

We believe opportunity becomes meaningful when people are prepared to recognize it, use it responsibly and build something from it. Preparation therefore goes beyond academic knowledge.

We encourage preparation of the mind through learning, critical thinking, communication and confidence; preparation of practical abilities through skills, entrepreneurship and a DIY mindset; and preparation of the spirit through faith, character, hope and a sense of purpose.

Our approach is based on learning by doing. People are encouraged to ask questions, practice what they learn, create solutions, share knowledge and learn from their experiences and from one another.

We also believe that support should strengthen people rather than create permanent dependence. We therefore seek to connect community members with partners, opportunities and resources while helping them develop the capacity to stand, contribute and eventually support others.

Through this approach, we hope to move from learning to action, from action to confidence, and from confidence to meaningful participation in the community.

Prepare the mind. Strengthen the spirit. Develop the hands. Be ready to serve.

06 — OUR VALUES

We believe in people, possibility and the responsibility to serve.

LAMPSTAND is guided by dignity, hope, learning, creativity, self-reliance and service. We believe every person has value and deserves the opportunity to discover, develop and use what they carry within them.

08 — COMMUNITY & SELF-RELIANCE

We are becoming who we are because someone opened a path for us.

LAMPSTAND comes from a refugee community that has experienced displacement, limited opportunities and the challenge of rebuilding a future. But our story is also about people and organizations who believed in our potential and helped create opportunities for us to learn, grow and serve.

Through support, training and opportunities from partners including UNHCR, Alight and others, members of our community have gained knowledge, confidence and practical experience. We have learned that meaningful support can build capacity, not only respond to immediate needs.

Yet many people remain behind simply because they do not know where opportunities are or how to reach them. Even today, some members of our community are unaware of learning opportunities such as the coding school within the camp. We therefore see communication as a way of creating pathways between people and opportunities.

We have also seen the potential already within our community. Students, entrepreneurs, young people, single mothers and artists are carrying abilities that can remain inactive when encouragement, exposure and opportunity are missing. Through opportunities provided by Alight, our artists were able to present and sell their paintings, showing what can happen when a door is opened.

We want to carry that experience forward. Self-reliance, for us, means turning support into knowledge, skills, confidence and opportunities that create value within the community. We want to move from receiving opportunities to helping create them for others.

We therefore welcome partners who believe in community-led solutions and are willing to invest in people, ideas and pathways toward greater independence. We know what it means when someone opens a door for you. We want to help open the next door for someone else.

From support to capacity. From opportunity to self-reliance. From receiving a path to opening one for others.
